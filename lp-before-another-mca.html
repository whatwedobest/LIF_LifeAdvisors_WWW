5 Questions to Ask Before Taking Another Merchant Cash Advance

When cash flow is tight, another advance can appear to be the fastest answer. Before adding a new obligation, understand the payment structure, total purchased amount, frequency of withdrawals, and how the new payment will interact with every existing MCA.

Read the Five Questions

A practical checklist

Use these questions to evaluate payment frequency, cost, affordability, contract terms, and whether new financing solves the underlying cash-flow problem.

  1. What is this advance actually covering—and for how long?
  2. What does the new payment cadence do to operating cash flow?
  3. How many MCAs are already in place, and how will another withdrawal overlap with them?
  4. What happens if revenue slows for 30, 60, or 90 days?
  5. Have you looked at options that are not another advance, including a review of the obligations you already have?

Can your business maintain its current MCA payments without taking another advance? If the honest answer is no—or you are not sure—it may be time to review the entire picture before taking on another obligation.

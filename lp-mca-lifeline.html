A Coordinated Path Forward

What Is the MCA Lifeline™?

The MCA Lifeline is Life Advisors’ consultative approach to evaluating and restructuring existing merchant cash advance obligations. It begins with the business, its cash flow, and every active MCA, then develops a coordinated strategy for working toward a more manageable position.

The MCA Lifeline is not another merchant cash advance and is not presented as a consolidation loan that automatically pays off existing MCAs.

01

Understand

We learn how the business operates, what changed, which MCA obligations are active, and where payment pressure is affecting the company now.

02

Develop a Strategy

We review balances, payment frequency, current status, business cash flow, and available information to outline a coordinated business-focused approach.

03

Work Toward Resolution

If the owner chooses to proceed, Life Advisors begins the restructuring process and helps the business navigate the agreed path forward.

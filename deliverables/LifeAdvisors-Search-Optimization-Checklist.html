Prioritized Search Optimization Checklist

SEO · AEO (answer engines) · GEO / AIO (AI search and citation) · SXO (search experience)

Structure for each item: Step → Client info needed → Implementation

Binding rule. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, team biographies, “day one” claims, or guaranteed payment reductions to complete this list. Those items stay off the site until the client provides verified, approved material. Optimization here means answering questions the site can already answer carefully, and making names, addresses, and policies consistent.
Priority 1 — Before launch Legal, identity, and on-page mismatches that search and AI engines will quote if left as-is.
Priority 2 — Highest-value content New or expanded copy using approved cautious language. Biggest lift for ranking and AI citation.
Priority 3 — After launch Proof, operations, and extras. Do not block the public site unless the client wants them first.

Priority 1 — Before public launch

Step Client info needed Implementation

Apply one consistent set across footer, consent language, JSON-LD (legalName / alternateName), contact card, and llms.txt so search and AI engines treat this as one company.

Apply counsel edits sitewide and in llms.txt. Do not add new service claims while waiting. Technical SEO tags stay; only the reviewed sentences change.

Priority 2 — Highest-value content (no new claims)

2.4 Add Contact FAQs for location, hours, and consultation terms Confirm the published hours, Boca Raton address, and that a consultation does not obligate enrollment (already stated on site). Add visible Q&A and FAQPage schema on contact.html: Where is Life Advisors located? What are office hours? Will a consultation obligate me to enroll?

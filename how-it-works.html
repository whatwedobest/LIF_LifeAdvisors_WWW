A Clear Process Begins With a Business-First Conversation

Every business arrives with a different story. Life Advisors begins by listening, reviewing the situation, and explaining the process in straightforward terms.

01. Listen

We learn what led to the current situation, how the business generates revenue, and what the MCA payments are affecting now.

02. Assess

We review the available business and obligation information, including active advances, payment frequency, balances, cash flow, and immediate concerns.

03. Strategize

We explain a proposed path based on the complete picture rather than applying a one-size-fits-all answer.

04. Advocate

If you choose to proceed, the team begins the restructuring process, communicates according to the approved plan, and helps you stay informed as the matter progresses.

If the program appears appropriate, the team develops a strategy around the current MCA obligations and the operating needs of the business. You should understand the proposed process, costs, responsibilities, potential risks, and realistic expectations before making a decision.

The initial conversation is confidential and does not obligate you to enroll.

About Life Advisors

Life Advisors helps business owners evaluate and restructure existing merchant cash advance obligations through a consultative, business-focused process.

Meet Timothy Shaw — Managing Partner

Leadership backed by a team

Timothy Shaw is the Managing Partner of Life Advisors and the company’s front-facing leadership spokesperson. His background in commercial banking, middle-market lending, credit analysis, business finance, and the merchant cash advance industry gives him a practical understanding of how lenders evaluate risk, how short-term financing can disrupt business economics, and why owners need clear guidance before making another financial decision.

Tim represents the perspective and standards of a larger Life Advisors team. He does not work alone, and Life Advisors is not a one-person consultancy.

The Life Advisors Team

Advisors and specialists behind every conversation

Behind every client conversation are advisors and experienced professionals who help evaluate the business, review current MCA obligations, explain available strategies, coordinate the restructuring process, and support the owner as the matter progresses.

Merchant cash advance pressure rarely affects only one line on a financial statement. It can influence payroll, inventory, vendors, employees, customers, and the owner’s ability to focus on running the company. The Life Advisors team works to make that complicated situation easier to understand without minimizing its seriousness or making promises no one can responsibly guarantee.

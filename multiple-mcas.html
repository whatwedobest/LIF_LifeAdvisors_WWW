Multiple MCAs require looking at the whole picture.

Stacked advances can quietly reshape cash flow. The work is coordination—not treating each obligation in isolation.

Why stacking changes the conversation

One MCA can feel manageable. Two or three can change how every operating day feels: deposits, vendors, payroll, and the reflex to take another advance.

Life Advisors treats multiple MCAs as a systems problem. The strategy should account for the broader situation rather than leaving previous obligations behind.

  • Map every advance against actual cash flow
  • Identify where payment cadence is constraining operations
  • Coordinate a path that keeps the business in view
